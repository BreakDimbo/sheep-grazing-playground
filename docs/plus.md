# Plus edition — plan

The faithful replica stays at `/`. The enhanced edition is `/plus/`: the same `main.js`
(it loads `plus/plus.js` only when `window.PLUS` is set, so the replica render is
unchanged, checked against the FINAL17 scores), plus physical-light additions. Each side has a small link to the other at bottom left.

Taste bar: restrained, cinematic, real light on real objects. No glow, sheen, particles,
glitch or chromatic aberration. Effects should be felt, not invisible.

| # | Feature | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Living light**: a slow day cycle. Morning, then noon (exactly the replica's light), a long golden hour, blue hour and a moonlit night. It runs through the replica's own sun and sky lights, so shadows are real cast shadows that lengthen and sweep across the board. Painted sprites, the river and the paper are graded by the light on the ground. Clouds stay sun-lit and turn rose after the fields fall into shade. There is a minimal clock and scrubber at the top. | shipped P1 |
| 2 | **Wind and weather of light**: soft cloud shadows drifting over the fields, projected along the sun. A gust field travels across the grass as a value ripple, crowns lean with the gusts, plus a wind ambience. | shipped P2 (cloud shadows, gust ripple, crown lean; sound later with #4) |
| 3 | **Flock**: cohesion around a wandering leader; three lambs (smaller sprites) trot after their mothers; a tap on open grass startles nearby sheep away (graded by distance, lambs fastest), they stop, look back and regroup; at dusk/night they lie down one by one to rest (legs tucked into the grass, bodies lowered) and get up again in the morning. | shipped P3 |
| 4 | **Camera as a lens**: tilt-shift miniature depth of field (thin-lens circle of confusion of the ground point under each pixel, i.e. a tilted focus plane; small dead band; the focus racks slowly to the flock, or the selected sheep), two separable 13-tap passes over the finished frame. A slow breathing orbit (±1°) after 6 s idle that eases out on any touch. Gyroscope parallax on phones (±3°, slowly re-centring; iOS behind a 'tilt to look' tap). Drift/parallax use a separate render camera; ray picks and projections follow it. ?tilt=0, ?drift=0 to compare | shipped P4 |
| 5 | **Sound** (muted by default, loads on first tap of the speaker): wind is generated pink noise in two beds left and right, with level and low-pass following the gust field that ripples the grass. Recorded bleats come every 10–30 s from a random sheep that isn't resting, panned to where it stands; a startle triggers them too; a lamb's bleat often gets its mother's answer. A recorded skylark sings in phrases by day, and a recorded field-cricket loop, doubled, detuned and spread, plays at night. Pencil-on-paper is grainy noise following drawing speed. Sources and licences in docs/sound.md. | shipped P5 |
| 6 | **Moonlit night**: a low moon beyond the far edge (about 20°), placed so its mirror image falls on the river. Moonlight is the key light, so sheep and trees cast long real moon shadows toward the viewer. Moonlight on the river: each fragment reflects the view ray off a slow, irregular swell normal; a soft ~3° lobe around the moon, scaled by water Fresnel, gives a calm broken column of light on the far reach (no sparkle, no glow). Scotopic colour: below about 0.3 ground irradiance, colour drains toward a cool grey and the exposure drops (in the lens pass; the CSS paper goes through the same transform so the ragged rim matches). Golden hour re-keyed: sky green ≥ red and green-grass bounce, so 18:00–18:50 is warm then neutral, no mauve. The moon rises with the end of blue hour (no dark dip at 20:00). | shipped P6 |
| 7 | **A passing shower** (`plus/rain.js`): about 15 s to build, 40–60 s of rain, 20 s to ease off. Overcast: the sun dims about 80% and the sky light greys (the `S.lightMod` hook, so sprites, paper and cloud shadows all follow). Wet ground: a multiply layer over the board by the ground's own normalised colour, so it gets darker and more saturated (deep green grass, dark brown paths), plus a faint additive Fresnel reflection of the grey sky, stronger in hollows; first drops darken blotches that merge until the field is evenly wet; the river is excluded. Cloud shadows by contrast only darken and cool. Rain through the lens (in the final lens pass): three depths of short motion-blurred streaks, far fine ones, mid ones and wide out-of-focus near ones, composited as small grey-lit drops (lighter over grass, darker over paper) plus a 7% scatter veil. Rings on the river: drops in 0.2 m cells, each a thin crest reflecting the sky with a darker trough inside, expanding at 0.2 m/s and fading in 0.55 s; they fade out on small boards (below ~60% of the 1440 board), where a ring would be a speck. Wet sprites: foliage a little darker and deeper, fleece darker and slightly grey, drying over ~1.5 min after. Huddle: the flock bunches up in the lee of the nearest tree (or together in the open), grazes less, and spreads out again when it clears. Drying: the edges of the sheet first, inward along a ragged noise front over about 55 s, with a darker tide line where the water draws back; the last wet islands are hollows in the middle. Sound: a recorded shower on leaves (CC BY 4.0), level and brightness follow the intensity; larks and crickets hush. Comes on its own every 3–7 min by day (06:30–19:12); the cloud button in the clock bar starts or ends one; `?rain=1` starts at once, `?rain=0` disables the automatic ones. Cost: one board overlay + one river overlay (9 cells per fragment) while active, streaks inside the existing lens pass. | shipped P7 |
